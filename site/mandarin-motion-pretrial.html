Static and single-play animated information cues

Compare unchanged information in a static reference against an optional, one-shot sequential reveal. No glyph ownership, identity colors, transcriptions or meaning claims are changed by the animation.

No study or evidence of learning gain. This is a prospective comparison stimulus, not an authorized participant trial. It collects no responses, stores no session state, and does not upload observations. Motion is never automatic; it is disabled when the operating system requests reduced motion. Animations do not depict measured tongue, lip, airflow, or acoustic trajectories.
