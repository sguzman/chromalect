Compositional sound-model feature glyphs

A constructed, monochrome notation for the 34 research-model units published by Zhao and Li (2009), with six independent slots in the model's C–V–V–V–C–T scheme. Outer shape represents consonant/vowel place; internal markings represent manner or vowel height; a small edge mark represents aspiration, voicing or rounding. The final independent stroke is the original citation-tone category.

Not a phonemic transcription of Chinese text. This only reconstructs selected source-model units from their assigned feature categories. The constructed examples below are not claimed to be attested Mandarin words or real speaker pronunciations. Human recognition, feature legibility at glyph size, phonemic inventory decisions, contextual IPA, and learning effects remain unverified.
