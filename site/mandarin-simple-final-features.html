Source evidence and contextual warnings

Source counts do not establish sounds. In particular, written \`i\` after sibilants, written \`u\` after palatal classes, and written \`o\` in certain labial contexts may not instantiate the default proposed vowel category. The underlying machine-readable ledger retains the original source frequencies, context cautions, and zero approval counters. See the research qualifications.
