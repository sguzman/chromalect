Mandarin · experimental color and meaning

Same glyph, three visual conditions

Directly compare an unmodified source-aligned Chinese stroke outline rendered in neutral ink, the same outline with its stable component identity colors, and the same colored outline with a provisional semantic pictogram. All conditions use one pinned character and geometry. Pronunciation and tone overlays are deliberately absent from this experiment.

This is a reproducible presentation comparison, not a human-subject experiment, semantic-family color scheme, or evidence of learning gains. Component identity colors are unchanged across conditions. The third condition is an unverified meaning proposal and correctly abstains when no pictogram was selected.
