Independent multilingual ontology · v1.0.0

Visual language ontology

A script-independent vocabulary for linguistic units, evidence, visual encodings and research specimens. Mandarin is the first implemented profile, not the limit of the project.

Human and machine references

Full human-readable ontology · Typed vocabulary JSON · Vocabulary JSON Schema · Source-grounded specimen graph · Instance JSON Schema.

Core vocabulary: 37 classes and 39 typed relations. Ontology coverage is broader than available implemented language datasets. Claims and interpretation status remain explicit.

Conceptual separation

Language & scriptWritten unitsPronunciation & meaningEvidenceVisual encodingSpecimens & evaluation

The writing system, graphic realization, sound, lexical interpretation, and display layer are separate objects. A visual encoding annotates rather than rewrites the original text.

First implemented example: 好 · hǎo

The IDS analysis ⿰女子 names 女 and 子 as distinct graphic components. Their v0.4 identity colors remain fixed inside the colored glyph; the spelling segments h and ao, and tone 3, belong to separately controlled presentation channels. The character also has the listed citation reading hào (tone 4). Stroke mappings are source-aligned, not independently visually certified.

Language and varieties 2 classes

Language

An identified language as a linguistic system.

LanguageVariety

A regional, social, or standardized variety of a language.

Writing systems and graphic structure 12 classes

Script

A conventional collection of written graphic signs; not a language.

WritingSystem

Conventions for using a script to represent a language or its units.

Orthography

A documented set of spelling and character-use conventions for a language variety.

Text

Preserved source writing with its original sequence of characters.

TextSpan

A source-addressable span of written text with explicit offsets.

Grapheme

A contrastive graphic unit defined relative to a writing system.

Character

An abstract written character; distinguished from Unicode encoding and a rendered glyph.

Glyph

A particular visual realization of a character or written unit in a font or drawing system.

GraphicComponent

A reusable graphic constituent with an explicitly defined identity and normalization policy.

ComponentInstance

A located occurrence of a graphic component inside a particular glyph or decomposition.

Decomposition

A source-attributed structural analysis; may have competing alternatives.

RadicalClassification

A dictionary or standard-specific indexing category, distinct from exhaustive graphic components.

Pronunciation and prosody 9 classes

OrthographicSegment

A token of a particular transcription or spelling system, such as a Pinyin initial or final.

Phone

A speech-sound realization in a specified phonetic context.

Phoneme

A contrastive sound unit of a particular language variety.

PhoneticFeature

An independently defined articulatory or acoustic property.

Syllable

A syllabic unit within a specified pronunciation analysis.

CitationReading

A documented out-of-context reading of a written unit.

ContextualReading

A pronunciation realized in a particular lexical or textual context.

ToneCategory

A defined tone category under a language-specific phonological analysis.

ProsodicFeature

A suprasegmental feature or realization, including stress, timing, intonation, or tone contour.

Morphology, words, and meaning 5 classes

Morpheme

A minimal meaning-bearing or grammatical linguistic form in an analysis.

Lexeme

An abstract lexical entry, not identical to a printed word token.

WordOccurrence

An instance of a word in context with optional source offsets and sense resolution.

Sense

A source-supported interpretation associated with a lexical unit in context.

SemanticFamily

A justified grouping of meanings or forms, not an identity color by itself.

Visual encoding and rendering 5 classes

EncodingScheme

A versioned policy mapping specified information domains into visual representations.

Palette

A versioned registry of color assignments and related accessibility metadata.

VisualAssignment

One typed mapping from a declared linguistic target to a visual value or symbol.

VisualLayer

A configurable channel connecting an information domain to an independently selected presentation mode.

RenderedSpecimen

A concrete rendered example with source, scheme, layer state, and review status.

Evidence and provenance 3 classes

Source

An identified external or internal evidence source with revision and licensing context.

Evidence

A traceable support item connecting a source to a bounded claim.

Claim

An asserted interpretation or observed result with epistemic status and support.

Experiments 1 classes

Experiment

A reproducible comparison of encoding conditions, hypotheses, and outcomes.

Profile data and validation

Existing sources include the 513-component palette, source-aligned geometry mapping manifest, and the Pinyin and tone presentation registry. The ontology identifies their roles and does not duplicate or silently redefine their values.

Other writing systems may have different units, different orthographic conventions, and different visual encoding schemes. No Mandarin-specific category is mandatory for all languages.
