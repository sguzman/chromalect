Mandarin initial categories

Compare 21 conventional Pinyin consonantal initial spellings with qualified broad IPA candidates and a reversible, monochrome feature-symbol prototype. The outer contour indicates a proposed place category; the central mark indicates manner; optional side marks distinguish aspiration. These shapes are experimental and have no independent human decoding, acoustic, or phonemic validation.
