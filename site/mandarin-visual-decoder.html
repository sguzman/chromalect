Decode a constructed visual citation code

Choose a source working initial and tone, then assemble an ordered final from the nine published geometric letter primitives. The decoder recognizes only the 673 distinct initial/final/tone combinations actually observed in the first 1,171 Pinyin citation readings. Each successful decode returns the original source spelling and example characters without proposing a phonemic transcription.

Literal orthographic control, not sound decoding. Geometric final primitives represent written letters, not vowels, codas or IPA. Initial categories are provisional working features; y, w and zero are orthographic conventions. No independent expert or human decoding trial, contextual reading or learning outcome has been established.
