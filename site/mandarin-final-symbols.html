Orthographic symbol control · nonphonetic baseline

33 written finals as geometric letter sequences

Nine arbitrary monochrome symbols encode the individual letters of published Pinyin final spellings. This deliberately nonphonetic control has 33 unique, reversible strings. The shapes have no assigned articulatory features: they do not show vowel quality, phoneme count, nasalization or syllable timing.
