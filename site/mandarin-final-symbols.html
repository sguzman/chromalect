33 written finals as geometric letter sequences

Nine arbitrary monochrome symbols encode the individual letters of published Pinyin final spellings. This deliberately nonphonetic control has 33 unique, reversible strings. The shapes have no assigned articulatory features: they do not show vowel quality, phoneme count, nasalization or syllable timing.

Written Pinyin finals versus published IPA options

This cross-reference pairs all 33 source-written finals and their arbitrary geometric letter-symbol sequences with candidate IPA headings in a published university diction guide. It does not transcribe a specific syllable: written i, o, u, uan, un and ong have multiple candidate reference headings, and other forms can require spelling normalization. Only six ordinary final tokens have provisional, uncertified single-vowel feature shapes.
