Color registry

Every color used by component identity, Pinyin spelling, and citation tone is specified in a versioned registry. Each channel has its own meanings; matching colors across channels do not imply matching linguistic meaning.

Component identities

The active v0.6 component registry contains 520 distinct identities, extended additively from the immutable 513 v0.4 assignments and their v0.5 five-component extension. No older identity changes shape or color. The site uses the original Chinese Make Me a Hanzi SVG outlines for direct stroke rendering, not browser font geometry. Source-aligned first-tranche coverage is 1,000/1,000, with automated candidates pending independent visual review.

Phonetic presentation

Pronunciation colors currently encode orthographic Hanyu Pinyin, not IPA or a verified featural phonetic alphabet. In Direct mode, the left edge is the initial, right edge is the final, and the top edge is the citation tone. Marker mode shows colored Pinyin and a shape-coded tone. The initial/final terminology refers to spelling decomposition, not always an actual initial consonant and phonetic rime.

Here, y and w are written initials, ∅ means no written initial, and the neutral tone has code 0. Contextual pronunciation and tone sandhi are not inferred.

Scope and reproducibility

These assignments are experimental and have not been established as beneficial for learning. The machine data is the source of truth, and the repo and website color keys are checked against it. Stroke paths are assigned component ownership using indexed source data, but stroke order is not a teaching channel.
