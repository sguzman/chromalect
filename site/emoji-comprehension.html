Blind emoji comprehension worksheet

Can a reader recognize a proposed meaning from the emoji alone? This local research worksheet hides the character and English meaning until an answer is committed. It uses Chromalect's 897 experimental expressions, not production-verified senses. Since the same emoji may be reused for several meanings, the default sampling policy shows any visible expression at most once per session.

Research boundary: Results stay in this browser session until you explicitly export JSON. Nothing is sent to Chromalect or GitHub. A self-rating is an exploratory observation, not independent comprehension certification or lexical approval. A separate protocol and independent participants are required before approving any mapping. Starting a new session discards unexported answers.
