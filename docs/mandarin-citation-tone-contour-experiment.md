# Mandarin citation-tone contour reference

This is a non-certifying experiment reference for Standard Mandarin **citation** tones. Chao's relative-pitch digits use 1 for low pitch and 5 for high pitch within a speaker's range. The conventional target shapes are T1 55, T2 35, T3 214 and T4 51. Neutral T0 has **no fixed context-free contour**; no fictitious shape or fundamental frequency is assigned.

Sources:

- [Hong Kong Polytechnic University BEPTH Basic Tones](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/bepth/introduction-to-phonetics/tones/basic-tones/?sc_lang=en)
- [Language Specificity in Speech Perception](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7077082/)
- [Phonetically Grounded Structural Bias in Learning Tonal Alternations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8350328/)

The four independent experimental presentation modes are plain tone label, monochrome contour, conventional symbol and pre-existing identity color. The machine-readable record uses the established five tone keys and does not modify colors, glyphs, phonetic transcriptions or any currently published character.

**Limit:** The full T3 dipping contour is a conventional citation form, not a claim about every connected-speech T3. Sandhi, speech rate, prosody, speaker pitch and other contextual realizations remain outside the current reference. A source-grounded visualization is not a human comprehension or learning study, and all experimental outcome counts remain zero.
